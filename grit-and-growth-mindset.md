# Grit and Growth Mindset

### Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.
 The video explains that success does not depend only on talent; it also depends on grit, which means having passion for long-term goals and perseverance, which means continuing to work even when things become difficult. Lastly, a growth mindset means believing that we can improve our abilities through continuous learning and effort.

### Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.
  This video explains the difference between a fixed mindset and a growth mindset. A fixed mindset believes that abilities are fixed, while a growth mindset believes they can be improved through effort, challenges, learning from mistakes, and accepting feedback.

### What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?
Internal locus of control means having a sense of responsibility that we are responsible for whatever we face in our lives. We can change our bad circumstances through hard work and discipline.
The key point in the video is that if you have an internal locus of control, you will stay motivated and face difficulties happily.
