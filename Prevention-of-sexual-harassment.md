# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

### **What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?**

- **Verbal:** Making bad comments, jokes, or asking for sexual favours.
- **Visual:** Showing bad pictures or sending wrong messages and emails.
- **Physical:** Touching, kissing, hugging, or blocking someone's path without their permission.
- Asking for sexual favours to give a promotion or other benefits.
- Asking someone for a date again and again even after they say no.
- Doing anything that makes someone feel uncomfortable or unsafe at work.

### **What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?**

- I will ask the person to stop if I feel safe.
- I will report the incident to HR or the office committee.
- I will keep messages, emails, or other proof of what happened.
- If I see someone facing this bad behaviour, I will support them.
- I will not ignore these actions and will report them immediately.
- I will help and cooperate during the investigation.
