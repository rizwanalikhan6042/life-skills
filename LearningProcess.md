## 1. Feynman Technique
If you are not able to explain a concept, it means you have not understood it.

## 2. Most interesting idea 
 The Pomodoro Technique is the most interesting idea I found in this video. It significantly improves our learning speed by aligning with the natural structure of our mind, which is       designed to focus intensely for short intervals before taking a break.

 ## 3. Active mode of thinking
 The high-concentration states in which our mind focuses on a topic with full attention.

 ### Diffuse mode of thinking
 A resting mode in which our mind makes new connections and generates ideas.


## 4. Steps to take when approaching a new topic
- Deconstruct the skill
- Learn enough to self-correct
- Remove barriers to practice
- Practice for at least 20 hours

## 5. The actions I am going to take to improve my learning process:
- Whenever I learn a new software topic or concept, I will ask myself: why do we use it? If we don't use it, what does it cost us?
- Instead of re-reading pages, I will look away from the page or code snippet and try to explain or recall what I just learned from memory.
- I will use the Pomodoro technique.
- I will block digital distractions.
