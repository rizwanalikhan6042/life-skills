## How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P.
I can use B = MAP to make new habits easier by making the habit very small and connecting it with something I already do daily.
M stands for Motivation, A stands for Ability and P stands for Prompt.
For example, if I want to develop a habit of exercising, I can do two push-ups after brushing my teeth in the morning. 
This way, I don't need much motivation because the task is very easy, and brushing my teeth reminds me to do it.
This method makes it easier to develop new habits without feeling that they are too difficult.

## Why it is important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of habit? (This is the most important concept in today's topic. Whatever you celebrate becomes a habit)
It is important to celebrate after completing a habit because it makes us feel good about what we have done. 
When we feel good, we are more likely to repeat that behavior again. Even if we do a very small task, celebrating it gives us a feeling of achievement and motivates us to continue. 
I think this helps us build habits without putting too much pressure on ourselves.

## What is the book's perspective about Identity?
The book says that instead of focusing only on what we want to achieve, we should focus on the kind of person we want to become. When we repeat small habits, we start believing that we are that kind of person. For example, instead of saying I want to learn coding, I should start thinking that I am a person who learns coding every day. 
Small actions help us build our identity over time.

## Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?
According to the book, we can make a habit easier by removing things that make it difficult to start. We should make good habits easy to do and reduce unnecessary steps. For example, if I want to study daily, I can keep my laptop and study material ready. 
This makes it easier to start studying without wasting time or effort.

## Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?
According to the book, we can make bad habits harder by adding more steps to them. For example, if I want to reduce mobile usage, I can keep my phone away from my desk while studying. 
This makes it harder to use my phone and helps me focus on my work.
