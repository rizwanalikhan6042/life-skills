# Listening and Communication Skills – Assignment 
## What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening? (Minimum 6 points)
- Focus and attention
- Be patient
- Show non verbal clues
- Take notes
- Ensure clearity
- Also notice body language

## According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? (Write in your own words, use simple English)
- focus on the conversation
- Pay full attention
- Respond properly
- Give time to speaker
- Notice emotions

## What are the obstacles in your listening process?

- **Distractions:** Mobile phones, noise, and other activities often distract me.
- **Overthinking:** I start thinking about my own thoughts instead of listening carefully.
- **Making assumptions:** Sometimes I judge or guess what the speaker is going to say before they finish.
- **Lack of interest:** If the topic is not interesting, I find it difficult to pay attention.

## What can you do to improve your listening?

- I can avoid distractions like mobile phones and noise.
- I can focus on the speaker and pay attention to what they are saying.
- I can avoid interrupting others while they are speaking.
- I can try to understand their point of view instead of making assumptions.
- I can ask questions if I don't understand something.
- I can be patient and listen until the speaker finishes.

 ## When do you switch to Passive Communication Style in your day-to-day life?

I usually switch to a passive communication style when I don't want to argue with someone or hurt their feelings. Sometimes I stay quiet even when I don't agree with something. I also do this when talking to elders or people in authority because I feel shy or uncomfortable sharing my opinions.

## When do you switch into Passive Aggressive communication style in your day-to-day life?

Sometimes when I feel angry or upset with someone, I don't say it directly. I may stay quiet, give short replies, or use sarcasm. This usually happens when I don't want to argue but still feel bad about something.

**How can you make your communication assertive?**

- I will try to speak clearly and share my thoughts.
- I will learn to say no when I don't agree with something.
- I will listen to others and respect their opinions.
- I will not be afraid to share my feelings.
- I will try to stay calm during disagreements.
- I will speak politely instead of getting angry or staying silent.
